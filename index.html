Approach

How I Make What I Make

01 — DESIGN THROUGH ITERATION

Good engineering rarely comes from the first concept. I explore multiple design directions, evaluating each through engineering principles, CAD, CAE and simulation before converging on a solution. The goal is not endless iteration, but knowing what to test, what to learn from each iteration, and when the design is ready to move forward.

02 — DESIGN FOR PERFORMANCE AND REAL-WORLD USE

A design is successful only when it performs beyond the CAD environment. I consider geometry, manufacturability, structural integrity, thermal behaviour, dynamics, material selection, tolerances, assembly and practical constraints together — ensuring the final solution is not just accurate in simulation, but viable to manufacture, integrate and use.

03 — ENGINEERING FIRST PRINCIPLES AS THE FOUNDATION

CAD is a tool; engineering reasoning drives the design. I build from first principles, using analytical calculations, kinematic derivations and physical understanding to establish why a design should work before validating it computationally. Every major design decision should be traceable to performance requirements, structural reality, physical behaviour or system-level constraints.

04 — DESIGN WITH THE COMPLETE CAE PICTURE IN MIND

Engineering disciplines do not exist in isolation. I approach design while considering the complete CAE workflow — FEA, CFD, multibody dynamics, thermal analysis, vibration, fatigue and other relevant simulations — understanding how changes in geometry or operating conditions affect each aspect of the system. This creates stronger feedback loops between design, simulation and validation, leading to solutions that perform reliably not just in one analysis, but across the entire engineering system.
