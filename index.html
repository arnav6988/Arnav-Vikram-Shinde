Projects

DRW-01 · CMP-SIM

CMP lens polishing machine — kinematic simulator

Avinya MilTech internship

A chemical-mechanical polishing (CMP) simulator for an oscillating overarm glass polishing machine. Modeled the oscillation arm as a four-bar mechanism to eliminate the quick-return effect, derived the top disc's induced RPM from combined arm oscillation and base disc motion, calculated disc profile geometry, and applied Preston's equation to predict material removal for production planning. Also built a predictive re-flattening algorithm to extend disc life, using Creo and MSC ADAMS, plus bearing selection to optimize machine life. Documented in a 23-page interactive LaTeX/PDF manual covering kinematics, contact mechanics, and surface topography.

DRW-04 · ENCL-MIL810G

Ruggedized enclosure — NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano

Mechanical design

A MIL-STD-810G compliant ruggedized enclosure for the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, built in AL6061-T6 aluminum over a 10-day design cycle.
